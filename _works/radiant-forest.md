A radiant exploration of the limits of reconstructing landscapes through images and measurement. The work presents a large-scale light installation that inverts the operation of a nineteenth-century photometer, transforming a measuring device into a system for producing images.

Work produced with the support of a 2024 Leonardo Grant for Scientific Research and Cultural Creation, BBVA Foundation. It is currently in production, and will be presented in 2027.
