Abelardo Gil-Fournier is an artist and researcher. His practice explores the relationships between matter, images, and knowledge through an ecological poetics in which notions such as visual culture or computation extend beyond the human realm.

His work often starts from historical cases and environmental processes, understanding images as traces of how matter, perception, and planetary change become entangled. His practice across different formats and contexts, from exhibitions and publications to lectures and collaborative research.
