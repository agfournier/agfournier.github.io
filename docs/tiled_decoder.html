Tiled Optical Decoder — Masked Solar Disk

Browser decoder for videos produced by encode_sun_to_carrier_video_masked_v1.py. It can crop the projected image from a larger phone-camera scene by selecting the four projected-screen corners.

1. Load required files

For best results, use manual exposure/focus lock on the phone. If decoding a camera view, the video should show the full projected rectangle.

2. Select projected-image corners

Click the projected image corners in this order: top-left, top-right, bottom-right, bottom-left. If decoding a clean screen recording with no surroundings, you can press “Use full video frame”.

3. Decode settings

“Frame step” should normally be 1. Use a larger value only if the recorded video has a higher frame rate than the encoded video.
